# Commercial Pilot Plan v2.5

## Participants

Include lecturers from multiple disciplines, a Head of Department, a module or programme coordinator, an Institution Administrator, an internal moderator and a temporary external reviewer.

## Required journeys

1. Institution onboarding and scoped user invitation.
2. Lecturer module assignment and module-context selection.
3. Bulk material upload with immutable versions.
4. Generic lesson generation with genuine sources where needed.
5. Assessment generation, safety review, edit and controlled export.
6. Internal moderation, changes requested and corrected resubmission.
7. External reviewer temporary access and expiry.
8. Departmental readiness, workload and handover oversight.
9. Analytics, Audit Centre, feedback and support request.
10. Tenant export and deletion preview.

## Exit criteria

- no critical security or tenant-isolation defects;
- no fabricated verified sources;
- no unauthorised assessment release;
- all role boundaries pass;
- agreed usability and quality thresholds pass;
- backup and restoration evidence exists;
- legal, privacy and institutional approvals are recorded.
