# Personas and Jobs to Be Done

## Lecturer
“When preparing and delivering a module, help me create, adapt, validate, organise and export teaching and assessment material so I save time without losing academic control.”

## Coordinator
“When coordinating modules, help me see coverage and alignment, identify gaps, harmonise shared material and follow up.”

## Head of Department
“When managing departmental teaching, help me allocate courses, review workload/readiness, appoint reviewers, monitor activity and act on risks without leaving the system.”

## Institution Administrator
“When operating the institution tenant, help me onboard users/structures, manage independent roles/policies, migrate material and preserve governance.”

## Moderator
“When reviewing an assigned assessment, give me only the authorised package, criteria, sources, annotation tools and a traceable submission workflow.”

## External Reviewer
“When conducting a temporary review, give me only assigned evidence and actions for the approved period.”
