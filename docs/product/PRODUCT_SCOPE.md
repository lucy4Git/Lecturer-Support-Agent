# Product Scope

## Product statement

For lecturers and authorised teaching-and-learning stakeholders who create, coordinate, review, moderate and manage teaching work, the Lecturer Support Agent is an AI-native multi-institution platform that performs those tasks through one conversational work area, unlike disconnected document generators or institution-bound tools.

## In scope

Conversational teaching/assessment generation; inline editing/version/export; generic and institution-aware responses with verified sources; course assignment; alignment; departmental oversight; moderation; temporary external review; permission-based bulk ingestion; configurable tenants/hierarchies; audit and lecturer-support analytics.

## Excluded

Student universal tutoring, research management, finance, HR, admissions, enterprise accreditation, general institutional administration unrelated to lecturer support and autonomous academic approval.
