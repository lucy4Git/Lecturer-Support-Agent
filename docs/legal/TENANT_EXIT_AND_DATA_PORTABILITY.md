# Tenant Exit and Data Portability

A tenant exit must preserve security and auditability while providing an authorised export of users, roles, structures, modules, documents and versions, conversations, outputs, review records, sources, settings and audit evidence. Export manifests must carry checksums and schema versions. After the agreed retrieval window, deletion follows the approved deletion workflow and remains blocked by legal holds. Provider secrets, platform security material and other tenants’ data are excluded.
