# Acceptable Use Policy Template

> Template only. Obtain legal and institutional approval before use.

Users must use the Lecturer Support Agent only for authorised teaching, learning, assessment, moderation and related institutional duties. They must not attempt to bypass access controls, retrieve another tenant's data, publish unreleased assessments, impersonate reviewers, upload unlawful content, introduce malware, fabricate evidence, or use generated work without required academic review.

Institutions must define permitted AI uses, confidential-data rules, assessment-release authority, copyright responsibilities, incident reporting and sanctions. The platform may suspend access, preserve audit evidence and notify authorised administrators when misuse is detected.
