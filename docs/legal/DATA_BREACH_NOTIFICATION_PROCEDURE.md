# Data Breach Notification Procedure

1. Contain the incident and preserve evidence.
2. Classify affected tenants, data, people and systems.
3. Revoke compromised credentials, sessions and integrations.
4. Notify the designated security, privacy and institutional contacts.
5. Assess legal/regulatory notification duties and deadlines.
6. Communicate verified facts without speculation.
7. Restore from trusted evidence and validate tenant isolation.
8. Record corrective actions, owners and closure evidence.

The procedure must be adapted to the applicable jurisdiction, institutional policy and contractual obligations.
