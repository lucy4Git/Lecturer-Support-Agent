# Terms of Service Template

> Draft operational template. It requires review and localisation by qualified legal counsel before use.

## 1. Parties and service

Identify the platform operator, subscribing institution, authorised users, service description, subscription term and order of precedence between the order form, service-level agreement, data-processing agreement and these terms.

## 2. Permitted use

The service supports authorised teaching, learning, assessment preparation, moderation and related academic operations. Users must comply with institutional policy, copyright, privacy, assessment-security and professional obligations.

## 3. Human academic responsibility

AI-generated outputs are drafts unless an authorised academic workflow records review, approval and release. The service does not replace lecturers, moderators, professional bodies or institutional decision-makers.

## 4. Prohibited use

Prohibit unlawful processing, unauthorised access, cross-tenant probing, credential sharing, malicious uploads, fabricated approval claims, student impersonation, and release of confidential assessment content without authority.

## 5. Content and intellectual property

Define ownership of institutional uploads, user inputs, generated outputs, platform software, templates and feedback. Require users to hold sufficient rights for uploaded materials. Do not promise exclusive rights in generic AI-generated content.

## 6. Third-party providers

Disclose configured model, infrastructure and communication subprocessors. Institution policy determines whether restricted data may be sent to a cloud model or must remain local.

## 7. Service levels and support

Reference the SLA, maintenance windows, incident response and support escalation procedure.

## 8. Suspension and termination

Permit proportionate suspension for security, legal or material breach. Provide data export and tenant-exit procedures, subject to retention and legal holds.

## 9. Warranty and liability

Use jurisdiction-appropriate provisions and exclusions. Do not exclude liability that cannot legally be excluded.

## 10. Governing law

Specify the contracting jurisdiction, dispute procedure and authorised notices.
