# Privacy Notice Template

> Draft template requiring legal and data-protection review for the deployment jurisdiction.

Describe the operator and institution roles; categories of account, teaching, conversation, file, audit, model-usage and support data; purposes and lawful bases; AI providers and subprocessors; cross-border transfers; retention; legal holds; security; automated assistance; data-subject rights; complaint channels; and contact details.

The notice must state clearly that advertisers do not receive institutional conversations through the Lecturer Support Agent and that API credentials are not stored in user-visible configuration.
