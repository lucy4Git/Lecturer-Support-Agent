# Data Licensing and Copyright

## 1. Rule

No dataset or full-text resource may be used merely because it can be downloaded. The project records the copyright holder, licence, source terms, permitted purpose, attribution, derivative obligations, commercial restrictions and expiry or revocation conditions.

## 2. Distinguish metadata from content

Bibliographic metadata may be openly reusable while the article, textbook, image or assessment remains copyrighted. The source service may store metadata and a link even when full text cannot be stored or used for model adaptation.

## 3. Licence decision matrix

| Licence/status | Discovery/index | Runtime retrieval | Commercial model adaptation | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Public domain/CC0 | yes | yes | generally eligible | verify jurisdiction and embedded third-party content |
| CC BY | yes | yes | potentially eligible | attribution and notice required |
| CC BY-SA | yes | yes | legal review | derivatives/share-alike implications |
| CC BY-NC/NC-SA | yes | limited | no by default | commercial product use requires permission/analysis |
| CC BY-ND | metadata/link | unchanged display only | no | no derivatives |
| Institution-owned | yes within tenant | yes within scope | no by default | institution approval required |
| Permission granted | per agreement | per agreement | per agreement | keep agreement reference |
| Unknown or all rights reserved | metadata/link | short lawful evidence only where permitted | no | obtain permission |

## 4. Repository-level caution

Repositories such as OER Commons contain resources under different conditions; each item must be checked. SkillsCommons commonly exposes CC BY resources, but embedded third-party assets and item metadata still require review. OpenStax and MIT OpenCourseWare currently use noncommercial/share-alike licensing for much of their libraries; they are therefore not assumed safe for commercial model training. Licence status can change across editions and must be captured per version.

## 5. Attribution record

For every reusable resource store:

- creator and contributors;
- title and version;
- source URL and persistent identifier;
- licence name and URL;
- copyright notice;
- required attribution text;
- modifications made;
- third-party exclusions;
- date verified;
- attribution placement requirements.

Attribution must follow the resource into generated derivatives and exports where required.

## 6. Institutional contributions

The data-sharing agreement must state:

- ownership remains with the institution or contributor;
- permitted product uses;
- whether content may be used for retrieval, evaluation or adaptation;
- confidentiality and assessment-security restrictions;
- duration and termination;
- return/deletion requirements;
- rights to generated derivatives;
- responsibility for third-party content;
- external AI provider processing terms.

## 7. User uploads

Uploaders confirm they have authority to submit the material. This confirmation does not transfer ownership and does not permit unrestricted model training. The system preserves uploader details and rights assertions but may require administrator or data-owner verification.

## 8. Generated outputs

Generated outputs must record model/provider, prompt and input provenance. They must not reproduce substantial protected text beyond permitted use. Users receive a notice that generated material requires academic and copyright review before publication.

## 9. Takedown and rights change

A rights complaint or licence change triggers quarantine, index removal, output trace analysis, notification to the data owner and a documented decision. Immutable audit metadata remains, while content bytes are purged where law or agreement requires.

## 10. Verified official references for the initial register

The acquisition register records official source terms verified on 2026-07-21, including OpenAlex CC0 data, broadly open Crossref metadata, DOAJ metadata under CC0, OER Commons item-level usage rights, SkillsCommons item-level Creative Commons metadata, and licence-filtered Europe PMC access. These references are planning evidence, not legal advice and not blanket permission for all content.
