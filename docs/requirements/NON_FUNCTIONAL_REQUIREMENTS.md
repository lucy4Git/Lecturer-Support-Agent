# Non-Functional Requirements

- **NFR-001 Security:** Least privilege, tenant isolation, secure sessions, encryption, secrets, scanning and audit.
- **NFR-002 Privacy:** Data minimisation, purpose limitation, retention, legal hold, export and archival.
- **NFR-003 Availability:** Define SLOs; initial production target 99.9% monthly excluding approved maintenance.
- **NFR-004 Performance:** Responsive local UI, prompt streaming and asynchronous long jobs.
- **NFR-005 Scalability:** Horizontal stateless scaling and tenant-aware partitioning/indexing.
- **NFR-006 Accessibility:** Target WCAG 2.2 AA.
- **NFR-007 Portability:** Modern Chromium, Firefox and Safari; desktop, tablet, mobile and installable PWA.
- **NFR-008 Reliability:** Idempotency, retries, queues, checksums, resumable ingestion and dead-letter handling.
- **NFR-009 Maintainability:** Typed contracts, modular boundaries, ADRs, tests, migrations and observability.
- **NFR-010 Auditability:** Immutable provenance across users, AI, sources, files, roles and reviews.
- **NFR-011 AI quality:** Measure usefulness, pedagogy, citations, unsupported claims, forgery and safety.
- **NFR-012 Cost:** Track model, retrieval, storage, ingestion and export cost by tenant/capability.
- **NFR-013 Internationalisation:** Locale, language, timezone, dates, grading, calendars and tenant terminology.
- **NFR-014 Interoperability:** Versioned APIs/events and appropriate educational/data formats.
