# v2.3 Acceptance Criteria

1. Six operations tables exist and are covered by tenant row-level security.
2. Job claiming is lease-based, concurrency-safe, retryable, idempotent, and dead-lettered after a bounded number of attempts.
3. Operations APIs are unavailable to Lecturer, Head of Department, Moderator, and External Reviewer roles by default.
4. Production configuration fails when rate limiting, malware scanning, or metrics protection is unsafe.
5. Uploads and archive members are scanned before storage when scanning is enabled.
6. Structured logs redact credentials and preserve request/correlation identifiers.
7. `/health`, `/ready`, and protected `/metrics` have distinct responsibilities.
8. Production Compose, Dockerfiles, and Kubernetes manifests contain no real credentials.
9. Backups generate a checksum manifest; restores require explicit destructive confirmation.
10. The onboarding example passes JSON Schema and semantic validation.
11. The synthetic corpus is deterministic, clearly labelled synthetic, and covers at least 10 disciplines.
12. All cumulative unit and release validators pass.
13. Runtime claims remain pending until the consolidated owner-machine report passes.
