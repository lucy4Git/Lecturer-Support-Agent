# v1.9 Unified Department Operations UX

Departmental operations remain contextual actions inside the single ChatGPT-style work area. The platform must not introduce a separate Head of Department portal.

## Context actions

The action rail displays only functions allowed by the active role:

- **Lecturer:** My teaching plan, session progress, module readiness, my workload, and handover.
- **Head of Department:** Department operations dashboard, calendar, teaching plans, readiness, workload allocation, and handover oversight.
- **Module/Programme Coordinator:** Scoped plans, readiness, workload, calendar, and handover management.
- **Institution Administrator:** Institution-wide calendar configuration only; no inherited Head of Department authority.

## Dashboard behaviour

The dashboard is an attention-management surface, not a vanity analytics page. It prioritises actionable issues: modules without plans, readiness blockers, missed sessions, overdue handovers, and overloaded lecturers. Each item must open the relevant scoped workflow rather than reloading a generic dashboard.

## Multi-device requirement

Forms and dashboards must remain usable on desktop, tablet, and mobile. Tables should collapse into accessible cards on narrow screens, keyboard focus must remain visible, and all status information must have text labels rather than colour-only meaning.
