# v1.9 Operational Scope Security

## Purpose

The departmental operations layer contains sensitive staffing, workload, delivery, readiness, and continuity information. Access is therefore enforced by the active role selected at login, organisational scope, tenant context, and PostgreSQL row-level security.

## Controls

- Institution Administrator and Head of Department remain independent roles.
- A lecturer can manage teaching plans, readiness, and handovers only for active module assignments.
- Workload summaries expose a lecturer's own workload or require scoped academic workload-review permission.
- Academic calendar management is separately permissioned from departmental operational authority.
- Handover acceptance is restricted to the assigned incoming lecturer or an authorised academic manager.
- Every version, status change, evidence update, and workload allocation produces audit evidence.
- All v1.9 tables are tenant-owned and included in the existing row-level-security policy application.
- No external moderator or external reviewer receives general departmental operations access.

## Fail-closed behaviour

Invalid lifecycle transitions, cross-tenant identifiers, unassigned lecturer access, missing permissions, and malformed date ranges are rejected. The system does not infer authority from job titles or from another inactive role assignment held by the same person.
