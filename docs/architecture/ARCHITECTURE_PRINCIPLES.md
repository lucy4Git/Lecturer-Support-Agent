# Architecture Principles

1. Tenant isolation before convenience.
2. Independent roles with scope- and context-aware authorization.
3. One experience with multiple hidden internal capabilities.
4. Generic AI by default; verified evidence where required.
5. Immutable content and append-only provenance.
6. Asynchronous, resumable processing for long work.
7. Versioned APIs, events, prompts and schemas.
8. Explicit, auditable human approval.
9. Configurable academic structures; no institution-specific hard-coding.
10. Security, accessibility, observability and cost are product features.
