# v1.8 End-to-End Moderation Scenarios

1. Lecturer submits an assessment for review.
2. HOD creates an internal moderation cycle for the current version.
3. Assigned moderator can open the task; an unassigned moderator receives 403/404-safe denial.
4. Moderator accepts, starts, records findings and submits a recommendation.
5. HOD records changes required; lecturer responds and creates a new version.
6. Resubmission creates round 2 and a new sealed pack while round 1 remains unchanged.
7. External moderator repeats the flow with a valid scoped grant.
8. Wrong-scope, expired and revoked grants fail on read, finding and submission actions.
9. Institution Administrator cannot record the academic decision.
10. Cross-tenant users cannot discover or access the cycle, task, pack or findings.
11. Approved output follows release and student-copy safety controls.
12. Responsive desktop, tablet and mobile interfaces retain the same single-work-area experience.
